About This Data

This analysis examines authorship patterns in NBER (National Bureau of Economic Research) working papers from 2005 to 2025. The data shows a clear and consistent trend toward increased collaboration in economics research, with the average number of authors per paper rising from 2.13 in 2005 to 3.22 in 2025.

Data source: Official NBER working paper metadata from the National Bureau of Economic Research.
