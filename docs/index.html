About This Data

This analysis examines authorship patterns in NBER (National Bureau of Economic Research) working papers from 2005 to 2025. The data shows a clear and consistent trend toward increased collaboration in economics research, with the average number of authors per paper rising from 2.13 in 2005 to 3.22 in 2025.

Notable observations include the significant jump in 2020, which saw both more papers published (1,713 vs. typical ~1,100-1,200) and higher collaboration rates. This likely reflects increased research activity related to the COVID-19 pandemic, which often required interdisciplinary collaboration. The upward trend has continued post-pandemic, with average coauthorship now exceeding 3.2 authors per paper.

Data source: Official NBER working paper metadata from the National Bureau of Economic Research.
