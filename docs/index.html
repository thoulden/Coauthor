About This Data

This analysis examines authorship patterns in NBER (National Bureau of Economic Research) working papers from 2015 to 2021. The data shows a clear trend toward increased collaboration in economics research, with the average number of authors per paper rising from 2.67 in 2015 to 3.06 in 2021.

Notable observations include the significant jump in 2020, which saw both more papers published (1,713 vs. typical ~1,200) and higher collaboration rates. This may reflect increased research activity related to the COVID-19 pandemic, which often required interdisciplinary collaboration.

Data source: Paper metadata from the nberwp R package, which compiles and cleans official NBER metadata. The underlying data is published by the National Bureau of Economic Research.
